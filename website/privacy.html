Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026

Clove does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking and no server of its own. Everything it shows is read on your Mac, and stays there, except where you connect a service yourself, as described below.

What Clove reads on your Mac, and why

WhatWhy, and where it goes
Calendar eventsTo show your upcoming events and remind you before a meeting. Read with your permission through macOS; never stored or sent anywhere.
What's playing in Music or SpotifyTo show the track and its controls. Read with your permission (Automation). For Spotify, the album artwork is downloaded from Spotify's own image servers, the same way the Spotify app does.
Battery and performance figuresTo show the System page and the charging and low-battery activities. Read only while shown; never stored or sent.
Headphones' batteryIn the downloaded version, to show each earbud's and the case's battery when your headphones connect. Read with your Bluetooth permission; never stored or sent.
Volume and brightness keysIf you let Clove handle them, it receives only the volume, mute and brightness keys (with your Input Monitoring and Accessibility permission). It cannot see anything you type.
What you copyTo show your clipboard history, only once you set Clove to Always Allow under Privacy & Security › Paste from Other Apps. Kept in memory while Clove runs. Items you pin are saved in Clove's own folder on your Mac until you unpin them. Nothing is sent anywhere, and items that password managers mark as secret are skipped.
Files on the shelfOff until you turn it on. Clove keeps a reference to files you drop on the notch; the files stay where they are and are only sent if you AirDrop them.
Apple RemindersOff until you turn it on. To list your reminders, tick them off and add one. Read with your permission; never sent.
AI coding agentsDownloaded version, off until you turn it on. If you connect one, Clove adds a hook to that agent's own settings file, and the agent tells Clove on this Mac what it is doing and which commands it asks to run. Nothing is stored or sent. Disconnect removes the hook.
Other apps' notificationsDownloaded version, off until you turn it on and allow Full Disk Access. Clove reads Notification Center's list on this Mac to show new alerts from the apps you choose. Nothing is stored or sent.
Terminal and ChromeDownloaded version, off until you turn it on. The front Terminal tab's last lines and Chrome's tab titles and addresses, read only while the Apps page is open. Private Chrome windows are never read; nothing is stored or sent.
Update checksDownloaded version, off until you turn it on. Asks GitHub for this project's releases once a day.
MicrophoneOnly while you record a voice note. The audio is transcribed and understood on your Mac and is never saved or sent.

Linear, if you connect it

If you paste a Linear personal API key into Clove's Settings, Clove keeps it in your Mac's keychain and uses it only to talk to Linear's API (api.linear.app): to read the issues assigned to you, and to make changes from a voice note after you have reviewed and approved them. That information goes between your Mac and Linear only, and Linear's own privacy policy applies to it. Disconnecting in Settings deletes the key.

What Clove stores on your Mac

Your settings, and a few small notes such as which deadlines it has already reminded you about and the names of your Linear projects and labels (so voice notes recognise them). They stay in Clove's preferences on your Mac. Deleting Clove removes them.

Apple

Voice notes use Apple's on-device speech recognition and Apple Intelligence models; nothing is sent to Apple for them. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS, Apple may send Clove's developers anonymous crash reports.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you visit. The site uses no cookies or analytics; it asks GitHub's public API for the number of stars and the latest download, and remembers your light or dark choice in your browser.

Children

Clove collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Every earlier version is in the project's history on GitHub.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/sriramkansala/clove/issues.
