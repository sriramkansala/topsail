koi pond — pixel study

Your Blender pond, re-rendered without its koi, then pushed through a diamond lattice: each "pixel" is a cell of a grid turned 45°, ink lines run wherever two colours meet, sixteen colours, horizontal brush streaks and ordered dithering. The koi are your cut-outs, sampled into the same grid every frame.
