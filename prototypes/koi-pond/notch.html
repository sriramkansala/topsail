koi pond — in the notch

Real sizes, 1 CSS px = 1 pt. The island keeps Topsail's shape and radii: 6 / 14 closed, 14 / 24 open. The black rounded block in the middle stands in for the camera housing; on a Mac, whatever the island draws there is hidden by the hardware.

A · idle: now and then a koi swims under the notch

The closed island grows two 60 pt wings, a koi crosses from one side to the other (out of sight under the camera for a few seconds), and the wings fold away. Each wing is open water from your render with the tip of a lily pad at its far end. In the app this would come every few minutes; here it repeats every few seconds.
